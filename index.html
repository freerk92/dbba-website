What we do

The Dutch Blood Bowl Association aims to increase the awareness and availability of Blood Bowl in the Netherlands and to grow the Dutch community.

We collect all available information about Dutch Blood Bowl organisations, leagues and tournaments in one central online place and share it with everyone who wants to play. Every form of the game is welcome: Blood Bowl, Sevens, Dungeon Bowl, Street Bowl, Blitz Bowl and more.
